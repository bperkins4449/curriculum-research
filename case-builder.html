Teaching Case Builder

Add the strip, EKG or photo, then write what you see in your own words. The builder lays your read out as a full Daily Images case: the vignette, the choices, why each wrong answer is wrong, the teaching point and the two AI reads. You fill it in, then keep it, fix it or cut it, and approved cases fill the residents' calendar three a day. On this site the builder does not draft with AI; the three examples show what a finished draft looks like.

House rules for the drafts

A checklist for writing cases. Your cut reasons add rules here, and you can write your own.

1 · You say the read

Talk the way you would at the board: what it is, what you want them to learn, the trap they fall into.

2 · Lay out the case

One button sets up the vignette, choices, why-not notes and teaching point from your take. Any field you mark as yours stays as you wrote it.

3 · You approve

Nothing reaches residents without your yes. Approved cases are ready for the Daily Images game.

What residents see

Day 1

This is the game's flow: pick, say how sure you are, lock it in, then see an AI read (or none) and decide whether to change. Use the switch on each card to try all three versions a resident might get.

Approved cases in the Daily Images format

Each item carries what the game needs: the image, the stem and choices, the correct answer, a right AI read and a wrong AI read (each with the choice it lands on), and the teaching point. It also keeps the Daily Images editor's own fields (prompt, correctIndex, teaching_point, source_note, scheduled_date).
